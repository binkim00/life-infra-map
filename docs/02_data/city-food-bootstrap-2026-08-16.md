# Seoul/Busan cafe and restaurant bootstrap — 2026-08-16

## Cause and source

The PostgreSQL database had only 3 Seoul cafes, 5 Seoul restaurants, 2,459
Busan cafes, and 1 Busan restaurant. The SEMAS 2026 Q2 nationwide commercial
store snapshot was already staged as `SourcePlaceRecord`, but almost none of
those rows had been materialized as searchable `Place` rows.

2026-06-30.

## Service-category filter and identity

`materialize_commercial_places` uses the source industry field, not AI or a
place-name guess. Cafe materialization accepts the SEMAS minor type `카페` and
rejects study cafes. Restaurant materialization rejects alcohol-only businesses,
bakeries/dessert-only classifications, convenience stores, cafeterias, and
non-food anomalies. A restaurant-classified row whose name claims `카페`,
`커피`, or `다방` remains a category conflict rather than being guessed into a
new category.

Eligible rows were Seoul cafe 22,739, Seoul restaurant 92,967, Busan cafe 7,335,
and Busan restaurant 35,097. The import was validated at 10, 100, and 1,000 rows.
Existing Places are linked only when the normalized name is exact and either the
normalized address is exact or coordinates are within 30 m. Similar nearby
names remain ambiguous. No Place is deleted or merged.

- same-SEMAS identity links: 1,433 source rows
- ambiguous eligible rows left unlinked: 1,389
